Opening Script Strip Architecture

The horizontal strip across the top holds the 15 scripted plays you committed from the Script builder:

  • Active Play Details: The top strip displays the active scripted play with its play number (e.g. 1 / 15), formation, set, play name, and tactical notes.
  • Sequential Execution: Clicking Ran it on the active play card moves to the next scripted play.
  • Formation Clustering: Scripted plays are sequenced in groups of 2–7 plays from the same anchor formation ("same look, different play"), letting you read how defenses react to specific personnel.
  • Safe In-Game Edits: You can open Script builder mid-game to tweak remaining plays; saving keeps your current play index.

15 Situational Cards Grid Architecture

The lower matrix displays 15 distinct situational cards spanning the field:

  • Down & Distance: 1st & med, 2nd & med, 2nd & long, 3rd & med, 3rd & long, 4th & short.
  • Field Territory: Backed up (<10 yd line), Red zone (<20 yd line), Goal line (heavy sets/compressed).
  • Coverage & Pressure Counters: Blitz beater (screens/mesh/quick), Man beater, Cover 3 beater.
  • Scheme Specials: Shot (verticals/deep shots), Quick game (3-step rhythm), PA / motion (play-action/jet).
  • Per-Slot Playlist: Clicking Ran it replaces only that card from that card's per-game shuffled deck without altering other cards.

Anchor Formation Clustering Engine

Real NFL offensive coordinators do not jump randomly between 13 different formations in the opening drive. Gridiron OC clusters the 15-play script into 3 to 4 anchor formations:

  • Same Look, Different Play: Automatically pairs complementary plays from the same personnel grouping (e.g. Inside Zone + PA Boot + Quick Slants + Bubble Screen from Gun Bunch Open TE).
  • Cluster Depth: Allocates 2 to 7 plays per anchor formation, giving you authentic diagnostic ability against defensive alignments.
  • Cooldown Protection: Auto-Fill avoids plays used in your last 1–2 opening scripts (cooldownIds), guaranteeing fresh plays every game.

Offense Navigator & Manual Editing

Fine-tune every slot in your opening sequence with granular controls:

  • Defensive Playbook Scout: Tapping Auto-Fill 15 engages the background 32-team scout, analyzing your opponent's base front (3-4 vs 4-3), playcalling identity (Blitz-Heavy, Disguise, Zone/Man), coverage shells, and weaknesses to bias counter-concepts.
  • Clean Opening Start: Starting a fresh game initializes with an empty sequence (0/15 plays), ready for one-tap auto-fill or manual selection.
  • Instant Search & Add: Filter the entire team catalog by formation, set, concept, or play name, and click any play to append it to your queue.
  • Drag-and-Drop Reordering & Deletion: Click and hold any play row to drag and drop it directly into any slot (#1–#15) in a single motion with visual insertion highlights and automatic edge scrolling. Locked plays stay locked with their play, and unused plays can be removed with ✕.

Slot Dynamics During Style Shifts

Selecting a play-call style updates situational cards dynamically while preserving continuity:

  • Fitting Cards Stay Put: If a currently displayed play already conforms to the newly chosen style, it remains on screen without unnecessary visual disruption.
  • Non-Fitting Cards Reshuffle: Only cards that violate the newly selected style draw a replacement play from that slot's per-game playlist.
  • Fallback Safeguard: If a situational slot has no play matching the chosen style, it retains its normal valid play.

Persistent Game Memory & Show Counts

Switching styles does not wipe your active game history:

  • Show Count Integrity: The board-wide MAX_GAME_SHOWS = 2 limit continues to track all called and displayed plays seamlessly.
  • Independent From Opening Script: Changing play-call styles alters only situational cards; your opening script strip remains untouched.
  • Infinite Adjustability: Switch styles as often as desired as game momentum swings (e.g. from Balanced in Q1 to Kill Clock in Q4).

Board-Wide Freshness & Variety Limits

Gridiron OC enforces strict variety rules across every single game:

  • Shuffled Slot Playlists: At Start Game, each of the 15 situational cards generates a shuffled per-game playlist from all plays fitting that card.
  • Max 2 Shows Per Game: Across the entire board, no individual play appears more than twice (MAX_GAME_SHOWS = 2) while any other fitting play remains.
  • No Duplicate Cards: Plays currently visible on another card are skipped while an unused fit remains.
  • Season-Wide FIFO Deferral: Plays shown across recent games are accumulated (up to 1,000 plays) and deferred to the back in chronological order (deferIds), prioritizing unseen plays first at the front of every card shuffle so the entire playbook surfaces across a 17-game season.

100% Playbook Coverage Guarantee

Every single play in your team playbook is tactically accessible:

  • Madden 27 Offensive Fit: All 19,105 offensive plays across all 32 team catalogs fit into at least one situational card.
  • Madden 26 Offensive Fit: All 13,793 plays are fully mapped and normalized.
  • Exhaustion Reshuffle: When a card's unused plays are exhausted, that card reshuffles its fitting pool for another pass.
  • Full Playbook Access: Your team's complete playbook catalog is accessible and ready to be called across matching situations.
